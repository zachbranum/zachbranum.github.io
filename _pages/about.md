I am a senior Communication major at Virginia Tech, concentrating in professional communication and finance, with a minor in Event Management, set to graduate in May 2027. This portfolio covers my four years at Virginia Tech, from COMM	1015: Communication Skills my first communication class as a freshman, through CMST 4714: The Human Communicator in an Era of AI, the capstone course I am finishing this semester. It exists to answer a question, What does a human communicator actually do that an AI cannot?

My short answer is a human communicator does something much deeper than interpreting and transmitting information. They bring lived experience, emotional investment, and an understanding of what it means to be human. AI can remarkably generate language, interpret text, and solve complex problems. AI may change the way humans communicate with each other for the rest of civilization but it will never take away our personal connections, thoughts, creativity, and emotions. In this fast pasted. hanging world we live in today it is so important that we keep learning, educate ourselves on this new technology, practice using it, and understand the power that it has. Your skills and knowledge in AI could be the difference in you getting you a job over someone else.
